# Phase 154 Browser Checklist

## Weekly Exam — required
Test at 320px, 360px, 390px, 430px, tablet and desktop.

1. Open a real started weekly-test attempt.
2. Pass the Before You Start screen.
3. Verify Previous, Next, Report and **Final Submit** are all visible at the bottom of the question card.
4. Scroll a long question/textarea; Final Submit must remain reachable without opening a hidden sidebar.
5. Tap Final Submit and confirm the modal shows Answered/Unanswered counts.
6. Tap Review Again; test must remain open.
7. Tap Final Submit again, confirm, and verify result redirect.
8. Verify autosave and timer continue working.
9. On 320–360px confirm labels do not clip.
10. Verify Start Test Now and Cancel are usable on the entry screen.

## Home YouTube Videos
1. Add/publish 2–3 videos in Admin > Videos.
2. Open Home.
3. Verify the YouTube section appears with one card visible at a time.
4. Use left/right arrows and dots.
5. Swipe horizontally on a phone.
6. Tap Play Video and verify the YouTube iframe loads only then.
7. Move to another slide; previous video/audio should stop.
8. Check Admin Settings video title/subtitle and YouTube channel link.
9. Verify Home initial loading remains fast before any video is played.

## Primary mobile-action smoke check
Open Student Login/Register, Admission, Spoken Materials, Learning Roadmap, Roadmap Lesson, Weekly Test Center, Weekly Result and Student Dashboard and confirm primary CTA buttons remain visible and tappable.
